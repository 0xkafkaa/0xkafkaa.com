Welcome to my little corner of the internet! I am a hobbist programmer and a postgrad ML student based out of PondicherryPondicherry is a little slice of France tucked away on India's southeastern coast, where Tamil culture meets French charm., currently studying in Paris.

This website has gone multiple iterations over the years and is a constant work in progress. This is my Digital Garden. The term was popularised by Mike Caulfield in his 2015 keynote essay The Garden and the Stream: A Technopastoral

I fell in love with the character Kafka from Kafka on the Shore by Haruki Murakami and hence the pseudonym if you are curious.
